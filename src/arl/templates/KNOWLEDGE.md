# What we know about the problem

A distillation, not a log: what the work so far has shown about the problem
itself. Brought up to date at every introspection by revising and merging, never
by appending. Each statement names what it rests on and belongs in the section
that says how sure it is. This is what an agent should know before it designs
the next experiment. How to work here is in `LESSONS.md`; what could be tried
is in `IDEAS.md`.

## Established

Shown by more than one experiment, or by a check that could have failed.

## Likely

One experiment, or a pattern not yet tested directly.

## Ruled out

Tried and failed, with the reason it failed, so that nobody tries it blind again.

## Open questions

What is not known and would change what we do.

## How the evaluator behaves

What the numbers react to, how noisy they are, traps in reading them.
