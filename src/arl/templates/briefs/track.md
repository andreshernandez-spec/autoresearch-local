There is no active track. A track is one approach on its own branch and worktree.

  {arl} track new <name> [--from <ref>]
Then write research/tracks/<name>.md: the approach, the premises that make it worth the
time, and the results that would end it. Once experiments have been judged, a new track
comes out of the latest closed review (--review <id>, decided "branch" or "pivot") or from
a person at a terminal. A parked track is reopened the same way: {arl} track set.
